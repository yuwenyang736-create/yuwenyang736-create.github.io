About

I’m a Drifting Artist…

Yuwen Yang works as a theatre researcher, director, playwright, and stage manager. His research focuses on Asian diasporic studies and translation in transilingual theatres, and his artistic practice centers on cross linguistic and cultural storytelling. He had presented his work at American Society of Theatre Research, Comparative Drama Conference, and American Theatre of Higer Education. His recent directing credits include Musical Fanletter(2024), and Her Rage (2025). His original script Her Rage had been staged in Los Angeles and New York. He also works as the stage and technical management in both China and America, with Wisconsin Union Theatre, Madison Opera, University of Wisconsin Madison Opera, University of Wisconsin Madison Theatre, Aranya Theatre Festival, China Children’s Art Theatre, and Drum Tower West Theatre.
